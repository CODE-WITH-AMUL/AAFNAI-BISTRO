About

A neighborhood cafe in Sinamangal

Aafnai Bistro is a neighborhood cafe and casual dining spot in Sinamangal, Kathmandu, serving coffee, snacks and everyday meals in a relaxed setting.

Whether you are stopping by for breakfast before work, meeting a friend for lunch, or winding down with dinner, there is a seat for you. The cafe is located in Sinamangal, on the Tribhuvan International Airport side of Kathmandu — convenient for a meal or coffee before or after a flight.

Service options include dine-in, takeaway and food delivery.
